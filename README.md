# Medical Writing & Regulatory Communication Portfolio

This is an independent portfolio designed to demonstrate competence and gain experience in regulatory labelling, scientific communication, and clinical trial data translation.

The purpose of this repository is to showcase independent medical writing completed in accordance to European Medicines Agency (EMA), UK Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA), and EU Clinical Trials Regulation (EU CTR) standards.

---

### Portfolio Objectives

**Regulatory Compliance:** Compliance with regulatory guidelines, focussing on **EU CTR Annex V** for lay summaries of clinical trials and **Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC)** frameworks.

**Data Integrity & Clarity:** Translating complex results, scientific language and statistics into Plain Language Summaries (PLS) that are accessible to the general public without maintaining scientific accuracy.

**Comparative Regulatory Analysis:** Evaluating authorized indications and safety warnings across competing therapeutic products via the Electronic Medicines Compendium (emc).

---

### 2. Comparative SmPC Audits
These regulatory analyses evaluate differences in official product information using the UK **electronic Medicines Compendium (emc)** database.

* **[Audit-01] Second-Generation Antihistamines (In Progress)**
  * **File:** `comparative-smpc-audits/Audit_01_Cetirizine_vs_Loratadine.md`
  * **Focus:** Comparison of Section 4.1 (Indications) and Section 4.7 (Effects on ability to drive and use machines).

* **[Audit-02] Biologic Monoclonal Antibodies (Planned)**
  * **File:** `comparative-smpc-audits/Audit_02_Biologics_Comparison.md`
  * **Focus:** Analysis of Section 4.3 (Contraindications) and Section 4.8 (Undesirable Effects / Black Triangle status).
